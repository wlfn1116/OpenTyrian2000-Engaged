## Findings worth keeping in mind

- The desync log format (`--- local frame N, hashed fields ---`) made the wallet diff trivial
  to spot across the two machines' logs. Keep both logs; pair the `rollback frame N` blocks.
- Wallet-only divergence with matching enemies/pools/rand means a payment rule differs, not an
  event: look for session flags or local-index payouts, not for missing kills.
- `eliteState` is not part of the enemy canary hash; a tier disagreement surfaces only when a
  bounty pays. Adding it to the hash would catch modifier drift a frame after it happens.
- Everything a level pays must route through a player-indexed, both-machines path;
  `endlessCashCredit`/`endlessEconomyIndex` are outpost-only by contract (doc/notes.md).
- The bounded state hash changes whenever the rollback registry grows; verify sim-identity by
  removing only the registration before regenerating `testing/replay_fixtures.tsv`.
